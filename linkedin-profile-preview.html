About

HVAC-R product development leader with 9+ years designing rooftop units, heat pumps, and chillers across Blue Star, Johnson Controls, and now Bosch Home Comfort USA. I take refrigeration engineering problems from concept through to manufacturable, field-proven products. Along the way I've been granted 10 US patents spanning refrigerant leak prediction via machine learning, heat pump performance optimization, condenser fan control logic, and hot gas reheat systems — recognized with Johnson Controls' Patent of the Year (2023) and a JCI Techathon 2023 win. As Technical Lead at Bosch Home Comfort USA, I'm driving product development for light-commercial low-GWP rooftop units and heat pump rooftop systems. My work spans the full development lifecycle: concept generation and Pugh matrix trade-off analysis, refrigeration system component design and selection using simulation tools to compress development time, design/peer reviews for technical and quality compliance, and leading cross-functional teams to hit schedule, training, and customer satisfaction goals. I hold an M.S. in Thermal Engineering (9.8 GPA) from Walchand College of Engineering and a B.E. in Mechanical Engineering (Distinction) from N.K. Orchid College of Engineering & Technology. Open to connecting with HVAC-R engineers, product development leaders, and anyone working on next-generation refrigeration, controls, or low-GWP technology — feel free to reach out.
