## 7. Traffic & Visibility Playbook

**Profile mechanics (do these first — they're one-time fixes with outsized impact):**
- [ ] Professional headshot (clear face, good lighting, neutral background)
- [ ] Custom banner image related to HVAC-R/industrial refrigeration (see the HTML mockup for a
  placeholder layout)
- [ ] Custom LinkedIn URL: `linkedin.com/in/anand-dulange` (drop the random ID suffix)
- [ ] Turn on "Open to Work" → target roles: Senior Refrigeration Engineer, Lead HVAC-R Engineer,
  Refrigeration Project Engineer (visible to recruiters only if you prefer discretion)
- [ ] Add your city/region — location strongly affects local recruiter search

**Ongoing visibility habits:**
- Post 1-2x/week: project photos (with permission), lessons learned, industry news commentary,
  energy-efficiency tips, "day in the life" of a refrigeration engineer
- Comment thoughtfully on posts from HVAC-R companies, industry groups, and connections — this
  shows up in more feeds than your own posts do early on
- Use 3-5 relevant hashtags per post: `#HVAC` `#Refrigeration` `#IndustrialRefrigeration`
  `#HVACR` `#EnergyEfficiency` `#FacilitiesManagement`
- Send a short personalized note with every connection request — mention a shared interest or their
  work in refrigeration/HVAC
- Join and stay active in LinkedIn Groups for HVAC-R, ASHRAE, or industrial engineering
- Request recommendations from former managers/colleagues — 3-5 strong ones significantly boost
  profile trust and search ranking

**Why this matters:** LinkedIn's search and "All-Star" profile scoring both reward complete
profiles, keyword density in headline/About/skills, and regular activity. Doing the checklist above
plus posting consistently is what actually drives profile views and recruiter InMails — not just
having good copy.
